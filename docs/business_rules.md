# Business Rules & Rating Multipliers
- Property Base Rate: 0.0200, Auto Base Rate: 0.0350, Cyber Base Rate: 0.0250
- Straight-Through Processing (STP) Auto-Approve: Loss history <= 1, Credit Score >= Product Min, Sum Insured <= 10M, Asset Risk Score <= 6.0
- L1 Underwriter Referral: Missing risk data, Credit Score 550-Product Min, Loss count = 2
- Senior VP Referral: Sum Insured > 25M, Loss count > 2
- Hard Decline: Credit Score < 500, Sum Insured > Product Limit
